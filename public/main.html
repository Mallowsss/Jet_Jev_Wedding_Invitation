Our Love Story

J

Jethro (Jet)

He is a man of patience and sincerity, consistently showing his dedication through his daily efforts to spend time with me. He always does his best to make me happy and willingly adjusts out of love and consideration.

Though I first knew him as someone quiet, I later discovered his playful, cheerful, and joyful nature. Above all, he is deeply family-oriented—and most importantly, he loves Jehovah, which is one of the qualities I admire most in him.

J

Jeverlyn (Jev)

Jeverlyn is the kind of person who brings me comfort and peace. She is caring and deeply committed to her family, and she has shown me the same love and attention—especially in times when I am unwell. With her, it feels easy to be genuine, because I know I will never be judged.

She also has a playful and affectionate side. She enjoys teasing, yet she remains one of the most caring people I know. Through the qualities I have seen in her, I have come to realize more clearly that she is the one I want to spend my life with. Because of this, I have grown to love her even more—and I will continue to love her deeply.

She carries a quiet confidence, as if she truly knows who she is. Her laughter is contagious, and even the simplest moments become special when I am with her. Most of all, she loves Jehovah—and that is what I admire most about her.

How We Met

On January 29, 2022, Jet’s mother asked Jev to add Jet on Facebook so he could join their bike rides. At the time, Jev did not initiate any conversation, as they were not yet personally acquainted, though Jet had already begun noticing him.

Although Jev would occasionally see Jet during Christian meetings, he only became more aware of him on October 15, 2023, during a youth gathering at Makati Circuit, when Jet kindly helped him with his belongings.

In January 2024, while organizing a congregational trip to Tagaytay, Jev messaged Jet to confirm his attendance. From there, their conversations gradually developed, including both personal and Bible-based topics. As their communication became more frequent, Jev set clear boundaries. On February 27, 2024, Jet admitted that he had long been observing him. Jev initially declined, feeling that Jet had not yet reached the spiritual standard he was seeking. Despite this, Jet consistently showed sincerity, even staying with Jev at the hospital until 4:00 a.m.

In December 2024, Jet formally asked Jev’s parents for permission to court him, which they granted.

On March 3, Jev gave Jet his answer after recognizing his sincerity and love for Jehovah. He had prayed that if Jet were truly meant for him, they would both serve as regular pioneers—and that prayer was answered.

On December 30, 2025, Jet proposed to Jev in a moment he would always treasure—carefully planned, filled with heartfelt words, and marked by quiet sincerity. As Jet spoke, Jev saw not only the man he had become, but also the love he had patiently shown all along. Overwhelmed with joy and certainty, Jev said yes.

"VERLYN found her THRO love"

And now, we invite you to celebrate this love with us.

Things to Do Nearby

Make the most of your visit to Manila

Rizal Park (Luneta)

Manila's most iconic green landmark — perfect for leisurely walks, picnics, and historical sights like the Rizal Monument

📍 ~300 m from venue

Robinsons Place Manila

One of Manila's largest malls with shopping, dining, cinemas, and entertainment all under one roof

📍 ~0.6 km from venue

Manila Bay Baywalk

Famous for breathtaking sunsets — stroll along the newly rehabilitated promenade with stunning views of Manila Bay

📍 ~0.8 km from venue

National Museum

Houses the National Museum of Fine Arts, Natural History, and Anthropology — a cultural treasure trove in the heart of Manila

📍 ~1.3 km from venue

Manila Ocean Park

A marine theme park featuring aquariums, antarctic exhibit, sea lion shows, and thrilling water attractions

📍 ~1 km from venue

Intramuros

Explore the historic 16th-century walled city, Fort Santiago, and Casa Manila — a living glimpse into Manila's Spanish colonial past

📍 ~2 km from venue

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our wedding day

What time should I arrive?
We recommend arriving 30 minutes before the ceremony starts at 2:30 PM. This will give you time to find the parking area, sign the guestbook, and get settled.
What is the dress code?
The dress code is Semi-Formal. We encourage guests to wear blue hues to complement our wedding theme — think soft blues and powder blues.
Is parking available at the venue?
Yes! But it is a first-come, first-served basis and limited slots only.
Can I bring a plus one?
Due to venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests formally invited on your invitation. Please check your invitation for the number of seats reserved under your name.
Will there be vegetarian/vegan meal options?
Yes, we will have vegetarian options available. Please indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies when you RSVP so we can accommodate your needs.
Are children welcome?
While we love your little ones, we've decided to make our wedding an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives parents a chance to enjoy a night off!
Can I take photos during the ceremony?
We kindly request an unplugged ceremony — no phones or cameras during the ceremony itself. Our professional photographers will capture all the special moments. After the ceremony, feel free to take all the photos you'd like!
What if I have dietary restrictions or allergies?
Please let us know about any dietary restrictions, allergies, or special meal requirements when you submit your RSVP. We'll work with our caterers to accommodate your needs.
Is there a gift registry?
Your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift we could ask for. However, if you wish to honor us with a gift, monetary contributions towards our future together would be greatly appreciated. A gift box will be available at the reception.
What is the hashtag for the wedding?
#VERLYNfoundherTHROlove — Please use this hashtag when sharing photos and memories from our special day!
Will there be accommodation recommendations?
We recommend staying at hotels near the venue in the Makati or BGC area. Popular options include Fairmont, Makati Shangri-La, and The Peninsula Manila. Please book early as June is peak wedding season!
What happens if it rains?
Our ceremony and reception are planned for indoor spaces at City Garden Suites, so we're covered rain or shine!
